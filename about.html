Our Culinary Story

Where Sourdough Pizzeria Meets Gourmet Burger Craft

Founded with a simple conviction: fast food doesn’t have to mean compromised ingredients. At Crust & Co, we take timeless baking traditions and artisanal frying techniques to create unforgettable dining moments.

The Secret in the Dough

48 Hours of Slow Cold Fermentation

Unlike commercial fast-food pizza dough rushed with artificial yeast, our dough is mixed with unbleached organic flour, spring water, sea salt, and a 5-year-old mother starter.

The dough rests in climate-controlled coolers for two full days. This breaks down complex starches, producing exceptional digestibility, complex aromatic flavor, and that iconic honeycomb blister when fired at 450°C.

The Golden Fillet

Real Whole-Muscle Poultry, Never Processed

Every zinger fillet starts with fresh, never-frozen whole chicken breast. We steep each cut in spiced cultured buttermilk for 12 hours so the meat remains tender and brimming with juice.

Upon order, it gets dredged by hand in our proprietary 11-spice flour coating and dropped into pure high-smoke vegetable oil for an ultra-crunchy, jagged golden exterior.
